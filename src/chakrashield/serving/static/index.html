Friction budget frontier

      
Each point is the full resolver with a Lagrangian shadow price λ added to every non-ALLOW action. λ is chosen on the validation split for the merchant's budget and applied unchanged on test. The floor is the conformally certified-RTO share, which is frictioned regardless of λ.

Model selection: weight temperature γ

      
w = costγ. γ = 0 is the unweighted accuracy model, γ = 1 full rupee weights. Early stopping on cost-weighted validation loss; the served model is the candidate with the highest resolver P&L on the validation split, never on test. Test numbers are reported for all candidates so the choice can be audited.

      

    

  

  

    

      
Model health: conformal drift monitor

      
Label-free. Every scored order lands in one conformal set; under exchangeability the mix is stationary and known from calibration. Sliding 5-minute windows (last hour) in the feature store; alarms on empty-set share, ambiguity shift, certified-RTO burst and PSI of calibrated p. Live from /v1/monitor/drift, refreshed every 10 s.
